About

Making indie games since 2019

2019

Pyrebug Studios is an independent game studio founded in Connecticut, now based in Brooklyn. It began in 2019, when a group of undergraduate students decided to stop talking about experimental game ideas and start shipping them.

Since then, Pyrebug has released four titles on Steam. RUBICON and Scrapshoot are couch multiplayer party games. Scrapshoot started life as a game jam entry. EOS-503 is a roleplaying game about resolving conflict through conversation, built with the University of Connecticut's Neag School of Education. Chromatic Isle is a short, free music puzzle game set across the four seasons.
